About

My Story

I'm a recent graduate with a degree in Finance and a minor in Business Analytics, currently working as a Data Analyst at BMW. I built my analytical foundation through prior roles at FanDuel and New York Life, and I'm now pursuing my Master's degree while staying close to the tools shaping the field, especially AI and how it's changing the way we work with data.

Throughout school, I'm an active part of the finance and accounting program, and today I co-founded BMW Link, a network built to connect interns and young professionals as they navigate the start of their careers. Wherever I am, I care most about turning numbers into decisions people can act on.

Outside of school and work, you'll usually find me outdoors. I love fishing, hiking, and playing sports like volleyball and soccer, and I've been skateboarding for years. You can usually find me either in the city or at the skatepark. I'm also a big fan of thrifting and always look for an excuse to explore somewhere new.
